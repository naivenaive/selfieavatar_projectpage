SelfieAvatar at a Glance

Method

  1. Selfie-style training data. We reconstruct synchronized multi-view captures and re-render them under selfie-style camera configurations to bridge the gap between controlled datasets and casual selfies.
  2. Camera and expression rectification. A Diffusion Transformer (DiT), trained with flow matching, aligns the three selfies to canonical viewpoints while matching the center image's expression. The standard three-view configuration uses azimuths of 0°, −45°, and 45°.
  3. 3D Gaussian head reconstruction. GS-LRM consumes the rectified images with predefined camera poses and directly predicts pixel-aligned 3D Gaussians. Training combines image reconstruction and perceptual losses to preserve geometry and facial detail.
  4. Expression-driven reenactment. An implicit expression encoder extracts motion cues from a driving image. Cross-attention injects these cues into multi-view diffusion, while multi-view attention maintains consistency across viewpoints. The generated views are reconstructed into an expressive 3D Gaussian avatar.
